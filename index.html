MERIDIAN No. 88

01

Write

Type on your physical keyboard or click the modeled keys. Shift selects the upper character; Caps Lock holds the type basket in its shifted position.

02

Return

Press Enter to sweep the carriage to the left margin. The return lever indexes the platen one line and feeds the paper upward.

03

Correct

Backspace reverses the escapement by one tooth. Like a real machine, it does not erase ink: type again to create an overstrike.

04

Inspect

Use the numbered views, orbit the camera, or enable Inspection Mode to open the shell and expose the universal bar, key links, escapement, and bell.

  1. KEY LEVERThe key descends and pulls a mechanical link.
  2. TYPEBARThe link accelerates one bar through the slotted segment toward the printing point.
  3. RIBBON VIBRATORA universal bail lifts fresh ribbon between the type slug and paper at the instant of impact.
  4. IMPRESSIONForce transfers a slightly imperfect film of ink through the fabric ribbon onto paper backed by the rubber platen.
  5. ESCAPEMENTThe universal bar alternates two dogs on a toothed wheel, releasing the spring-biased carriage exactly one pitch.
  6. RIBBON FEEDEach stroke winds a fraction of ribbon onto one spool. End eyelets reverse the drive before the inked length runs out.

The simulation is a fictional composite machine, but its action follows documented manual typewriter mechanisms.
