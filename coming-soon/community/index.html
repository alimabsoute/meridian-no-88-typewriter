PROPOSED PROFILE ANATOMY · OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED

Enough detail for a restorer.
Enough room for a life.

01

THE MACHINE

Make, model, year range, typeface, carriage width, mechanical condition, and repairs—with serial number visibility controlled by the contributor.

02

THE PROVENANCE

What is known, what is remembered, and what remains uncertain. Oral history is labeled as such rather than presented as verified fact.

03

THE IMPRESSION

A short sound recording and type sample may be added only when the contributor owns the media and chooses to share it.

04

THE KEEPER

Display name or pseudonym, neighborhood-level location, and an optional note about why the machine is still in use. No exact address is requested.

02 / CONTRIBUTOR CONTROL

CONSENT IS PART OF THE RECORD

Your machine. Your terms.

The intended workflow makes visibility a deliberate choice at every stage. Nothing would become public just because it was uploaded.

  1. 01

    START A PRIVATE DRAFT

    Add only what you want recorded. Saving a draft would not create a public page.

    PRIVATE
  2. 02

    SET EACH BOUNDARY

    Choose display name, neighborhood visibility, serial-number treatment, and which photos or recordings can appear.

    OWNER CONTROLLED
  3. 03

    REVIEW THE EXACT PAGE

    See the finished profile and consent summary before a moderator receives it for accuracy and safety review.

    PREVIEW FIRST
  4. 04

    PUBLISH OR TAKE IT HOME

    Publish only after explicit approval. Unpublish, revise, download, or request deletion later without explaining why.

    REVOCABLE

PROPOSED PRIVACY BASELINE

What the register should never require.

  • × Exact home or storage address
  • × Public legal name
  • × Visible ownership documents
  • × Public serial number
  • ✓ Pseudonyms supported
  • ✓ Neighborhood optional
  • ✓ Media consent itemized
  • ✓ Export and deletion path

03 / PROPOSED RELEASE PATH

LISTEN FIRST, THEN OPEN THE SHELF

A slow, local launch.

Community features should earn trust before they earn scale. The sequence below is a proposal, not a promised schedule.

6.1

LISTENING TABLE

Co-design the record.

Interview Philadelphia writers, repair shops, collectors, and first-time owners. Test what belongs on a profile and what should stay private.

PROPOSED · RESEARCH
6.2

INVITED PILOT

Publish a small first shelf.

Invite a limited group to create profiles with hands-on support, moderation, consent receipts, and a tested removal process.

PROPOSED · CLOSED PILOT
6.3

PUBLIC REGISTER

Open carefully.

Expand submissions only after privacy, accessibility, attribution, reporting, and moderation workflows have been independently reviewed.
