07.B

PROPOSED PUBLISH FLOW

A second decision at every threshold.

Publishing would be a separate ritual from writing. Closing, saving, or filing a sheet would never imply consent to share it.

01 / PRIVATE DESK

A saved page remains yours alone.

Writing stays in the private paper desk. There is no background upload, public draft URL, discoverable profile, or automatic training opt-in in this concept.

DEFAULT STATEPRIVATENot visible on the board

02 / REVIEW PROOF

Read the exact page that would leave the desk.

The review isolates manuscript text, title, formatting, content warning, and selected excerpt. A clear back action returns to the private copy without publishing.

TITLEThe Last El Before SnowVISIBILITYNot published

03 / IDENTITY & SCOPE

Your byline can be separate from your account.

Choose a pseudonym, category, reply preference, and visibility. Account identity would not appear unless you deliberately select it.

North WindowReplies offPublic board

04 / FINAL CONFIRMATION

Publish would be unmistakable—and reversible.

The final screen repeats the byline, visibility, and rights summary. One deliberate confirmation publishes. A later removal action takes the page off the board.

07.C

TRUST SPECIFICATION · DIRECTION

A public room with doors, rules, and receipts.

These are product commitments proposed for design and technical validation—not live policy or legal terms.

◐

PRIVATE / PUBLIC

Hard separation

Private drafts and board posts would be distinct records. Publishing creates a reviewed copy; it does not expose the private paper desk.

Aa

IDENTITY

Pseudonyms by design

Authors choose the byline shown on each post. Real names, email addresses, and account details stay out of the public page.

§

OWNERSHIP

The writer keeps the work

The intended model: writers retain copyright and grant only the limited, revocable display permission needed to show a post.

×

REMOVAL

Visible deletion controls

Remove from the board from the post or account area. Any narrow safety or legal retention would be stated plainly before launch.

!

REPORTING

Human-readable reports

Readers can report harassment, hate, plagiarism, threats, or private information and add context—without confronting the author.

↺

MODERATION

Review and appeal

Proposed decisions include a reason, action taken, and a path to appeal. New-account limits reduce abuse without ranking the writing.

PROPOSED MODERATION LOOP

Report → review → reason → appeal

  1. 01Reader reportsCategory + optional context
  2. 02Content is assessedUrgent safety routes first
  3. 03Decision is explainedAuthor receives a reason
  4. 04Appeal is availableSecond review where appropriate

07.D

RESPONSIBLE ROLLOUT

Earn the right to open the board.

Phase 7 depends on the future Phase 6 account and privacy foundation. The board should not launch before identity, deletion, and moderation are tested.

THE BOARD STAYS CLOSED UNTIL

  • Private drafts cannot be discovered through any public route
  • Publish consent is tested with writers using keyboard, touch, and assistive technology
  • Deletion, export, pseudonym changes, reporting, and appeals work end to end
  • Moderation coverage and response targets are staffed—not merely documented
